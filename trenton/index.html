Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Trenton

Many premature tank failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Drain and flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year to protect the heat exchanger.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually includes the manufacture date, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank models reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is beyond that point and also shows rusty water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement after a leak.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble needs only a flush, yet the same sediment, neglected for a few years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and shorten the life of the tank, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off. A quick call saves time, money, and cold showers.

Common Water Heater Problems in Trenton

Every type of water heater fails in its own way, and we work on all of them around Trenton. Gas tanks usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
