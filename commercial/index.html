Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Staff can't run the dish room without hot water that reaches the right temperature, and health inspectors check the handwashing sinks right away. We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork shows a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Hamilton Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Hamilton and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore hot water, and document the entire call for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Hamilton Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
